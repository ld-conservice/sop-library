In one paragraph

Share a functional preview of the eLearning course through Review 360 so stakeholders can confirm flow, structure, and accuracy before full development continues. Delayed feedback here has a direct timeline cost.

Purpose

To share a functional preview of an eLearning course (Rise 360, Storyline, or Review 360) for stakeholder testing. This lets stakeholders confirm flow, structure, and accuracy before full development continues — catching issues while they’re still cheap to fix.

When to use this

  • An early functional build exists and is ready for stakeholder eyes
  • You need confirmation before committing to full development

Before you start

  • Confirm Review 360 link permissions allow stakeholder comments
  • Do a pass through the prototype yourself before sending — broken links reflect on the whole review

Steps

  1. Verify the link

    Ensure the Review 360 link permissions allow for stakeholder comments.

  2. Compose the email

    Import the template below via the Gmail logo shortcut, or copy the content into a new message. Add your signature.

    i
    Note

    If the “To” line disappears when importing, click “Recipients” to bring it back.

  3. Log the CC

    Paste the item-specific Monday.com email address into Cc so the thread and all feedback auto-log to the project.

  4. Send

    Confirm the prototype link is functional, then send to stakeholders.

    📧 Email Template: Prototype Ready for Review
    ToPerson, Person, Person
    CcPerson
    Bcc—
    SubjectPrototype ready for review – [Project Title] – feedback due [Date]

    Hi [Name],

    The prototype for [Project Title] is ready for review. This version shows the early build so you can confirm flow, structure, and accuracy before full development continues.

    Please review the prototype and submit your feedback via Review 360 by [Date].

    [INSERT REVIEW LINK BUTTON/TEXT HERE]

    When reviewing, please focus on:

    • Flow and navigation
    • Accuracy of content
    • Tone, examples, and scenarios
    • Visual direction
    • Any changes needed before full build begins

    Please review the prototype by [Date/Time] and reply with one of the following:

    • I approve this prototype as-is.
    • I approve this prototype with the changes outlined in my feedback. No re-review is needed.
    • I do not approve of this prototype. Please send an updated link after revisions.

    What happens next: after feedback is received, updates will be made and the course will move to final approval.

    Note: delayed feedback may impact the overall project timeline.

    Thanks for your time and partnership,
    Instructional Design Team

Special cases

Feedback comes in past the stated deadline

Unlike the proposal and outline templates, this one has no silence-equals-approval clause — the note explicitly says delayed feedback impacts the timeline instead. Follow up directly rather than assuming approval, and flag the schedule risk to your Team Lead.
