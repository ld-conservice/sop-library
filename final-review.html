Final Review

In one paragraph

Once every prior round of feedback is addressed and the build reflects the finished course, send it to stakeholders for final sign-off. At this stage, feedback should be limited to critical issues only — not another round of preference tweaks.

Purpose

To gain final stakeholder approval before a course launches. This confirms all prior feedback has been addressed and the version under review is exactly what will appear at go-live.

When to use this

  • All prior review rounds (Content Outline, Prototype) are complete and resolved
  • The build is finished and ready for launch pending sign-off

Before you start

  • Confirm the Review 360 (or final delivery) link is fully functional
  • Confirm all prior comments are resolved and the version reflects the final build

Steps

  1. Verify the link

    Ensure the Review 360 (or final delivery) link is fully functional, all comments have been resolved, and the version reflects the final build.

  2. Compose the email

    Import the template below via the Gmail logo shortcut, or copy the content into a new message. Add your signature.

    i
    Note

    If the “To” line disappears when importing, click “Recipients” to bring it back.

  3. Log the CC

    Paste the item-specific Monday.com email address into Cc so the thread and all feedback auto-log to the project.

  4. Send

    Confirm the final link is correct, then send to stakeholders for approval.

    📧 Email Template: Final Review Ready for Approval
    ToPerson, Person, Person
    CcPerson
    Bcc—
    SubjectFinal review for approval – [Project Title] – response due [Date]

    Hi [Name],

    The final version of [Project Title] is ready for your review and approval. All prior feedback has been addressed, and this version reflects the completed training as it will appear at launch.

    Please review the course and submit any final feedback via Review 360 by [Date].

    [INSERT REVIEW LINK BUTTON/TEXT HERE]

    At this stage, feedback should be limited to:

    • Critical content inaccuracies
    • Functionality issues
    • Compliance or policy concerns

    Please reply by [Date/Time] with one of the following:

    • I approve this training as-is.
    • I approve this training with the changes outlined in my feedback. No re-review is needed.
    • I do not approve of this training. Please send an updated version after revisions.

    What happens next: once approval is received, the training will move forward to launch. Delays in approval may impact the scheduled go-live date.

    Thanks for your time and partnership,
    Instructional Design Team

Special cases

A stakeholder raises a preference-level change at this stage

The template scopes final-review feedback to critical inaccuracies, functionality issues, and compliance/policy concerns only. A stylistic preference isn’t one of those — redirect it to a future revision cycle rather than reopening this build.

Approval is delayed past the go-live date

Flag the risk to your Team Lead immediately. The template already warns the stakeholder that delayed approval affects go-live — use that as your basis for renegotiating the date rather than absorbing the slip silently.
