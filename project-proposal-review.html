Project Proposal Review

In one paragraph

Once the project proposal is drafted and Team Lead-approved, send it to stakeholders for formal alignment on goal, scope, and timeline. CC the project's Monday.com email address so the thread auto-logs, and treat silence past the deadline as approval to proceed.

Purpose

To gain formal stakeholder alignment on the project goal, scope, and timeline before development begins. This step confirms that every named role and Subject Matter Expert (SME) is actually committed to the project schedule — not just aware of it.

When to use this

  • The project proposal document is fully drafted
  • You need stakeholder sign-off before moving Project Phase to Design

Before you start

  • Complete the project proposal document in Google Sheets
  • Have your email signature set up and ready to attach
  • Know the item-specific Monday.com email address for this project

Steps

  1. Draft the proposal

    Complete the project proposal document in Google Sheets.

  2. Get internal sign-off

    Obtain Team Lead approval to confirm feasibility and alignment before this goes anywhere near a stakeholder.

  3. Compose the email

    Import the template below into Gmail using the Gmail logo shortcut, or copy the content into a new message. Add your signature via the pen tool.

    i
    Note

    If the “To” line disappears when importing, click “Recipients” to bring it back.

  4. Log the CC

    Find the item-specific Monday.com email address for this project and paste it into Cc. This auto-logs the entire thread, including replies, to the project record.

  5. Send

    Confirm every link is active, then send to the primary stakeholders.

    📧 Email Template: Project Proposal Ready for Review
    ToPerson, Person, Person
    CcPerson
    Bcc—
    SubjectProposal Review – [Project Name] – Action Required by [Date]

    Hi [Name],

    The project proposal for [Project Name] is ready for your review. Please take a few minutes to confirm the details and roles outlined below so we can move forward on schedule.

    [INSERT REVIEW LINK BUTTON/TEXT HERE]

    When reviewing, please focus on:

    • Key dates and deadlines
    • Assigned team roles and SME/stakeholder names
    • Alignment with your team’s capacity and availability

    If you are named as a stakeholder or SME, you are expected to participate fully and meet deadlines. If you will be unavailable, please assign a proxy or provide an alternate. If you cannot commit to this project, notify us immediately so we can adjust the project team before kickoff.

    Please review the proposal by EOD [Date] and reply with one of the following:

    • I approve this proposal as-is.
    • I approve this proposal with the outlined changes. No re-review is needed.
    • I do not approve of this proposal. Please send an updated link after revisions.

    Note: If we do not receive a response by the deadline, we will assume the proposal is approved and move forward with implementation.

    Thanks for your time and partnership,
    Instructional Design Team

Special cases

A named SME or stakeholder can't commit to the schedule

Per the template note, they should notify you immediately, before kickoff, so the project team can be adjusted. Don’t wait for this to surface mid-project — flag it the moment it comes up in reply.

No one replies by the deadline

Silence is treated as approval. Move the Project Phase forward and proceed with implementation — but keep the sent email in the thread as your record that the review window was offered.
