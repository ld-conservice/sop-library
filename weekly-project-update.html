Weekly Project Update

In one paragraph

Every Friday, send a short status email for any project in Needs Analysis, Design, Review, or Revise. It keeps stakeholders current, surfaces blockers early, and prevents the thread from going quiet between milestones.

Purpose

To maintain transparency and professional momentum by giving stakeholders a consistent, actionable summary of project progress every Friday. This prevents communication “black holes” and clearly identifies blockers before they become surprises.

When to use this

Send by EOD every Friday for any project currently in a Needs Analysis, Design, Review, or Revise phase.

Before you start

  • Know exactly what’s outstanding from the stakeholder and what you’re actively working on
  • Have the item-specific Monday.com email address ready for CC

Steps

  1. Confirm timing

    Send by EOD every Friday for all projects currently in Needs Analysis, Design, Review, or Revise phase.

  2. Compose the email

    Import the template below via the Gmail logo shortcut, or copy the content into a new message. Add your signature.

    i
    Note

    If the “To” line disappears when importing, click “Recipients” to bring it back.

  3. Log the CC

    Paste the item-specific Monday.com email address into Cc.

  4. Add team visibility

    Add design@conservice.com to Bcc — always Bcc, never Cc, for this line.

    i
    Note

    Bcc keeps the design distribution list informed of the project’s status without pulling the whole team into the stakeholder’s direct replies.

  5. Send

    📧 Email Template: Weekly Project Update
    ToPerson, Person, Person
    CcPerson
    Bccdesign@conservice.com
    SubjectWeekly Project Update – [Project/Course Title]

    Project: [Insert Project Title]
    Update: [Insert Date]

    What we need from you:

    • Review and provide feedback on [document name or course section] by [date]
    • Provide missing input for [e.g., audience, objectives, enrollment] by [date]

    What we’re working on:

    • [Course/script outline drafted and in review]
    • [Module built in Rise and awaiting content approval]

    Thanks for your time and partnership,
    Instructional Design Team

Special cases

There's genuinely nothing new to report this week

Send it anyway. The point of the cadence is to prevent a black hole — a short “on track, no change” note still counts, and keeps the thread from going cold between milestones.

The project just moved to Launch or is on hold

Stop sending weekly updates — the cadence covers Needs Analysis, Design, Review, and Revise only. See Project Completion & Wrap-up for what to send once a project actually finishes.
