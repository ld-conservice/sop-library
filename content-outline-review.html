Content Outline Review

In one paragraph

Before high-effort development like Rise 360 authoring begins, send the completed outline, script, or storyboard to stakeholders and SMEs to confirm instructional flow, objectives, and assessment logic all line up.

Purpose

To ensure stakeholders and SMEs agree on the instructional flow, course objectives, and assessment logic before high-effort development begins. Catching a structural problem here is far cheaper than catching it after the course is built.

When to use this

  • The outline, script, or storyboard is complete and saved in the project’s assets folder
  • You’re about to start full development (e.g., Rise 360 authoring)

Before you start

  • Verify the outline/script/storyboard is complete and saved in the assets folder
  • Have the review link ready and confirmed accessible

Steps

  1. Verify readiness

    Confirm the outline, script, or storyboard is complete and saved in the assets project folder.

  2. Compose the email

    Import the template below via the Gmail logo shortcut, or copy the content into a new message. Add your signature.

    i
    Note

    If the “To” line disappears when importing, click “Recipients” to bring it back.

  3. Customize

    Update every bracketed placeholder to match this specific project.

  4. Log the CC

    Paste the item-specific Monday.com email address into Cc so the whole thread and all stakeholder feedback auto-log to the project.

  5. Send

    Confirm the review link is accessible, then send to stakeholders.

    📧 Email Template: Content Outline Ready for Review
    ToPerson, Person, Person
    CcPerson
    Bcc—
    SubjectContent Review – [Project Name] – Action Required by [Date]

    Hi [Name],

    The course objectives and content outline for [Project Name] are ready for your review. Please take a few minutes to confirm accuracy, flow, and coverage before we begin full development.

    Items for review:

    • Course Objectives – the three primary goals for the course (high-level outcomes)
    • Learning Objectives – specific performance outcomes (note: these may shift slightly based on final assessment examples)
    • High-Level Outline – the basic roadmap and structure (not a full script)

    [INSERT REVIEW LINK BUTTON/TEXT HERE]

    When reviewing, please focus on:

    • Alignment with the approved proposal’s purpose and objectives
    • Accuracy of topics, modules, and learning outcomes
    • Completeness of scenarios, examples, and simulations
    • Fit for the target audience and role expectations
    • Any missing or unclear content areas

    Please review by EOD [Date] and reply with one of the following:

    • I approve this outline as-is.
    • I approve this outline with the following changes. No re-review is needed.
    • I do not approve of this outline. Please send an updated version after revisions.

    Note: If we do not receive a response by the deadline, we will assume the outline is approved and proceed with development.

    Thanks for your time and partnership,
    Instructional Design Team

Special cases

Learning objectives shift slightly after this review

That’s expected — the template itself notes objectives may change slightly based on the final assessment examples. A small refinement isn’t a re-review trigger on its own.

No one replies by the deadline

Silence is treated as approval. Proceed with development, but keep the sent email as your record that the window was offered.
