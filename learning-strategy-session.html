TL;DR
The Learning Strategy Session is where you translate the PM's Discovery Brief into an instructional strategy. Own the meeting, capture decisions in the update thread, and complete the required board fields to start your SLA clock and advance the project phase.

Purpose

Project Discovery answered the business questions. The Learning Strategy Session (formerly Needs Analysis) answers the learning questions: what instructional approach fits, what learners need to be able to do, and how you'll assess it.

The board treats the actual meeting date as the moment your SLA clock starts, so getting this phase right sets the tone for the rest of the project.

When to use this

  • The project has been assigned to you and Project Phase is set to Learning Strategy Session
  • The PM has completed the Discovery Brief and handed off to you
  • NA Meeting Required is set to Yes on the parent item
Note If NA Meeting Required is No, skip this article and go directly to Building the Project Proposal.

Before you start

  • Read the PM's Discovery Brief on the parent item's update thread
  • If the Discovery Brief has gaps that would affect your ability to build a learning strategy, flag them to the PM now. Don't wait to raise them during the session.
  • Confirm your access to the parent item and its subitems
  • Have the LD Naming Convention Master open (you'll need it if you create subitems)
  • Note the Prioritization Scorecard so you understand priority context going into the meeting

Steps

  1. Schedule the meeting

    Update NA Meeting Date (Scheduled) when the meeting is booked. This tells the board (and the PM) that the project is moving.

  2. Conduct the meeting

    Cover instructional strategy, learning objectives, delivery recommendations, learner experience, assessment strategy, and learning activities.

    Do not re-run business discovery. If the Discovery Brief has gaps, flag them to the PM instead of rebuilding the intake conversation.

    Tip Use your team's approved AI meeting recorder. It saves 20-30 minutes of note-taking and gives you a searchable transcript for the proposal draft.

  3. Send the recap

    Send a summary email to all attendees within 24 hours. CC the item-specific Monday email address so the thread auto-logs to the project.

  4. Complete the required board fields after the meeting

    Update all five on the parent item. Entering NA Meeting Date (Actual) is the trigger that starts your SLA clock and moves the project into Proposal.

    • NA Meeting Date (Actual)
    • Designer Complexity Score
    • Est. Project Hours
    • Project Target Due Date
    • Content Delivery Format
    Automation Once NA Meeting Date (Actual) is entered, the board moves the item to the Project Proposal group and starts your SLA clock. You never need to drag the item to the new group manually.

Special cases

The stakeholder cancels the meeting last minute

Update NA Meeting Date (Scheduled) to the new date. Do not touch NA Meeting Date (Actual) until the meeting actually happens. Your SLA clock has not started yet.

If the stakeholder cancels twice without rescheduling, flag to the PM so they can reassess readiness.

A rush request is active on this project

Check the Project Rush Requested column. Three rush types affect this phase differently:

  • Yes - Short notice request: proceed as normal, compress meeting scheduling to within 2 business days.
  • Yes - Stakeholder expedited: confirm rush budget with Team Lead before scheduling.
  • Yes - Compressed Timeline: run the Reverse Calendar tool with the rush budget applied before setting Project Target Due Date.

See Handle a Rush Request for the full workflow.
