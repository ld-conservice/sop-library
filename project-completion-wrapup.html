In one paragraph

Within 1 business day of a project wrapping, send a completion summary that confirms delivery, recaps outcomes, notes anything tabled for later, and invites the stakeholder to complete a short feedback survey.

Purpose

To guide instructional designers through properly completing and sending the final project wrap-up email. This ensures stakeholders receive confirmation of delivery, a summary of outcomes, and an invitation to give feedback for continuous improvement.

When to use this

Send within 1 business day of the project reaching Launch / completion.

Before you start

  • Pull the official project data from the Llamaland board — request summary, what was delivered, completion date
  • Note anything tabled or deferred so you can list it accurately
✓
Tip

Boost survey response rates with three tactics: mention the survey verbally during the final sign-off meeting so it isn’t a surprise; add a one-line personal thank-you if the stakeholder was especially involved; and remind them their input directly shapes how future requests get prioritized and resourced.

Steps

  1. Confirm timing

    Send the wrap-up email within 1 business day of project completion.

  2. Compose the email

    Import the template below via the Gmail logo shortcut, or copy the content into a new message. Add your signature.

    i
    Note

    If the “To” line disappears when importing, click “Recipients” to bring it back.

  3. Customize

    Replace every bracketed placeholder with the official project data from the Llamaland board.

  4. Log the CC and BCC

    Paste the item-specific Monday.com email address into Cc. Add design@conservice.com to Bcc — always Bcc, never Cc — so the team stays informed without joining the reply thread.

  5. Close the loop

    Verify the email appears in the project thread, then mark the Monday item as Complete.

    📧 Email Template: Project Completion Summary
    ToPerson, Person, Person
    CcPerson
    Bccdesign@conservice.com
    SubjectProject Finalized – [Project Name] – [Date]

    Hi [Name],

    We’re happy to let you know the project below is complete. Thank you for your partnership throughout!

    Project Summary

    • Request: [Brief summary of the original request]
    • Delivered: [What we created/delivered]
    • Completed: [Date]

    What you need to know
    [List any follow-up actions, notes, or tabled items, or things that might require a new submission in the future]
    Example: “We tabled X and can revisit it through a new form submission if priorities shift.”

    We’d love your feedback. We’re grateful for your collaboration. Your feedback helps us refine our process and better support your team’s goals.

    Could you please take 5 minutes to share your experience? Take the 5-minute survey

    Thanks for your time and partnership,
    Instructional Design Team

Special cases

Something from the original request was tabled or deferred

List it plainly under “What you need to know,” per the template’s example language: note what was tabled and that it can be revisited through a new form submission if priorities shift. Don’t let a deferred item just disappear from the record.
